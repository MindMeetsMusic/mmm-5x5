Why Mind Meets Music Matters

The Origin Story

A choir director asked students to write thank-you notes after a performance. Many couldn’t write them. That moment became the spark for Mind Meets Music.

The Outcomes

12.1 percentage point literacy gains. Achievement gaps reduced by 69% in Title I schools.

The Interruption

After reaching 15,000 students, Dr. Monique Salinas suffered a near-fatal accident. The program closed. Nine years later, she returned to rebuild it.

The Pilot

11 classrooms. 5 schools. 140 sessions. 92% implementation fidelity and outstanding teacher feedback.

The Scale

The digital platform can bring the same curriculum to more than 65,000 elementary schools nationwide.

The Number That Matters

$16.65 per student per year. Less than the cost of two school lunches.
